# Orbit Triage: user guide

Orbit Triage reads the company's security logs and tells you, in plain words, whether anything is wrong and what to do about it. You do not need a security background to use it.

## The eye

When you open the app, the eye checks the logs.

| Eye colour | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Blue**, with a sweeping light | Still checking the logs. |
| **Blue**, calm | Nothing suspicious was found. |
| **Amber** | Only minor problems. Keep an eye on them. |
| **Red**, pulsing | Something serious needs attention now. |

Under the eye, the headline says the result in one line, for example **"Something is wrong."**

## Asking a question

Type any question into the large box and press **Enter** (or the ↑ button). You can also tap a ready-made question such as **"Was any data stolen?"** or **"Who to call"**.

- Answers only use what is in the logs. Nothing is guessed or taken from the internet.
- The small grey tags in an answer are **sources**. Click one to see the exact log line it came from.
- Press **×** to close an answer.

## The problem cards

Scroll down (or press **See what we found**) to see one card per problem.

- **Critical / Serious / Moderate / Low** says how urgent it is, with a hint like **"Act right now"**.
- The **first sentence** says what happened in everyday words; the second says what it means for the company.
- **Tell these people** lists who must be told and how fast. Phone numbers and email addresses are links you can tap.
- **What to do** is a checklist. Tick each step as you do it; the bar shows your progress. Ticks are remembered in your own browser only.
- **See how it happened** and **Full report** open more detail.

The tiles above the cards give the totals at a glance and show **who to call first**.

**Checked and harmless** lists things that looked suspicious but were planned and approved by IT in advance. Nobody needs to act on them.

**What these words mean** explains terms like *phishing*, *VPN* and *port scan* in one line each.

## The other pages

| Page | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Details** | Every finding, split into what the logs prove, what is only suspected, and what the logs cannot tell us. |
| **Timeline** | Every log entry in time order. Click a row to see the original line. |
| **Story** | Each attack step by step. Solid boxes are proven; dashed boxes are educated guesses. |
| **Who is who** | Links the different names one person or computer has in different logs. |
| **Report** | The full written report. Download it or copy it to send to management. |
| **Ask** | A full chat page for longer conversations. |

## Times

All times are in **UTC**. Pakistan time (PKT) is UTC + 5 hours and is shown in brackets where useful.
